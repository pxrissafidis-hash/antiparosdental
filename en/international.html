International & French-speaking patients

Already on the island? Care for your smile here.

Visitors and residents of Antiparos and nearby Paros find modern dental care close by — from experienced dentists, in a language they understand. We work comfortably in Greek, English and French, for implants, oral surgery and aesthetic restoration.

How it works

Whether you are already here or planning your visit

Many patients find us while already on Antiparos or Paros for their holidays. Others, especially French speakers from Canada and across Europe, choose to combine their care with a peaceful stay on the island. Whatever your case, we keep the process clear.

01

First contact

Write to us with your request. We reply in Greek, English or French.

02

Remote assessment

Based on what you send us (e.g. X-rays), we give you an initial estimate and plan.

03

Planning your visit

We arrange your appointments in as few visits as possible and inform you of time and cost.

04

Treatment & follow-up

We complete your treatment on the island and remain available for follow-up.

Staying on the island

A peaceful retreat with Aegean views

For your stay, we can suggest accommodation in a serene Cycladic house on Antiparos, with stunning views over the blue Aegean and a sunlit courtyard — ideal for resting before or after treatment.

Accommodation is optional and is a separate service, with its own booking via Airbnb. We are happy to help with information and alternative options according to your needs.
