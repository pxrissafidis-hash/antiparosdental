International & French-speaking patients

Dental care in Antiparos, for patients from abroad.

Travelling for dental treatment? Please send us your radiographic examination before you arrive, and we will advise whether treatment is suitable. Consultations are available in Greek, English and French. If you are already on the island, we are happy to see you.

How it works

From first enquiry to your visit

Each case is assessed individually. A preliminary assessment does not replace an examination at the clinic.

01

First enquiry

Write to us with your request. We reply in Greek, English or French.

02

Preliminary assessment

Send us a cone-beam CT (CBCT) and photographs of your mouth. Based on these, we advise whether treatment is likely. The final decision is made at your first appointment.

03

Planning your visit

We arrange your appointments in as few visits as possible and inform you of time and cost in advance. We establish your budget together with you once we receive your enquiry.

04

Treatment & follow-up

We complete your treatment on the island and remain available for follow-up care.

Enquiries

Send us your enquiry

Please do not attach medical records or imaging to this form. After your first enquiry, we will advise you of a secure way to send them.

Sending your CBCT scan

To protect your medical data, please send your CBCT scan as a password-protected PDF to info@antiparosdental.com. Do not send the password in the same email. Share it separately by phone or WhatsApp to our number. We will confirm receipt.

Prefer to contact us directly?

You are welcome to reach us by whichever method suits you best. We reply in Greek, English and French.
