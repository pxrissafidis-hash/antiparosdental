Periodontology

Healthy gums, the foundation of every smile.

The gums and bone that support the teeth are just as important as the teeth themselves. Periodontology cares for their health — preventing and treating problems before they become serious.

[ Photo: clinic — to be added ]

Why gum health matters

Periodontitis is one of the most common causes of tooth loss in adults — and it often progresses silently, without pain in the early stages.

With regular check-ups and timely intervention, we can stop its progression and keep your natural teeth for many more years.

Prevention is the key

A regular periodontal check-up catches problems early, when treatment is simpler and more effective.

Signs to watch

When to visit us

Bleeding gums

Gums that bleed when brushing or flossing — often the first sign.

Swelling or redness

Gums that are red, swollen or tender to the touch.

Bad breath

A persistent unpleasant smell or taste that does not go away.

Receding gums

Teeth that "look longer" or sensitivity at the roots.

Loose teeth

Teeth you feel moving or shifting.

Sensitivity

Increased sensitivity to cold or heat near the gum line.

Frequently asked questions

Does periodontal treatment hurt?

Treatment is done under local anaesthetic where needed, to be as comfortable as possible. Most patients report minimal discomfort.

How often is a check-up needed?

It depends on your condition. We will suggest a personalised monitoring schedule.

Can periodontitis come back?

With proper oral hygiene and regular monitoring, the condition is controlled effectively. Consistency in care is decisive.
