Tooth restoration

Dental implants, with scientific precision.

A missing tooth is not only an aesthetic matter — it affects chewing, bone health and your confidence. With modern implantology we restore natural function and appearance, with a plan made for you.

Why an implant makes the difference

An implant is a titanium root placed in the bone that acts as the foundation for a new, stable tooth. Unlike other solutions, it does not burden the neighbouring teeth and keeps the bone alive.

Every case begins with thorough diagnosis and a personalised plan — because no two mouths are ever the same.

ITI Certified

Member of the International Team for Implantology (ITI) — one of the most respected international bodies in implantology, committed to evidence-based, safe practice.

Frequently asked questions

Does placing an implant hurt?

The procedure is done under local anaesthetic and most patients report minimal discomfort. We give clear instructions for a smooth recovery.

How long does the whole treatment take?

It depends on the case. We give you a realistic timeline from the first visit — especially important if you are travelling.

How long do implants last?

With proper care and regular check-ups, an implant can last many years. Oral hygiene is decisive.
