Invisible orthodontics

Straighten your smile, discreetly.

With clear aligners we straighten teeth without metal braces — an almost invisible, comfortable and removable solution, ideal for adults.

[ Photo: clinic / aligner — to be added ]

What clear aligners are

They are a series of transparent, removable trays, made specifically for your mouth. They are worn daily and changed at regular intervals, gradually moving the teeth into the correct position.

It is the same invisible-orthodontics technique many know by the commercial name Invisalign®. In our clinic we apply this approach with modern clear-aligner systems.

Who it suits

Ideal for adults with mild to moderate alignment needs. At the first assessment we will tell you honestly whether it is the right solution for you.

Advantages

Care network in the Cyclades

Children's orthodontics & complex cases

Clear aligners cover many adult cases. For children's orthodontics, as well as more complex orthodontic cases, we work closely with a partner orthodontist on Paros — so that every patient receives the right, specialised care.

Frequently asked questions

Is it the same as Invisalign?

It is the same invisible-orthodontics technique using transparent trays. Invisalign® is a commercial name; we apply this approach with modern clear-aligner systems.

How long does treatment take?

It depends on the case. At the first assessment we give you a realistic timeline tailored to your needs.

How many hours a day must I wear them?

It is usually recommended to wear them most hours of the day, removing them mainly for meals and brushing. We will give you clear instructions.
