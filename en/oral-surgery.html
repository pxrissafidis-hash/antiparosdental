Oral surgery

Wisdom-tooth removal, with confidence and calm.

The third molars — wisdom teeth — often cause pain, inflammation or pressure on neighbouring teeth. With meticulous planning and a gentle surgical technique, extraction becomes a safe and predictable experience.

When a wisdom tooth needs removing

Not every wisdom tooth has to be removed. But when it is impacted, partially impacted or causing problems, timely extraction prevents larger complications.

We assess each case with a clinical examination and X-ray, and explain clearly whether — and why — the procedure is recommended.

Experience that counts

The clinic places particular emphasis on wisdom-tooth surgery, with years of experience in Athens, Paris and Orléans — and patient safety as the first priority.

Frequently asked questions

Does wisdom-tooth extraction hurt?

The procedure is done under local anaesthetic so you feel no pain during it. Afterwards we give clear instructions and, where needed, medication for a comfortable recovery.

How long is the recovery?

Most patients are back to normal within a few days. We give you a realistic timeline — useful especially if you are travelling.

Do I need all four removed at once?

It depends on the case. Sometimes a staged approach is recommended. We will discuss the best option for you together.
