Photo guide

Photographs of your face and mouth, before the first assessment.

Photographs help us assess your aesthetics and function from a distance. Take them yourself with your phone, following the steps below.

Before you start

Basic conditions

Natural daylight from a window, no flash.

A plain, light background.

Camera at its highest resolution, with no filters.

Hair tied back and no glasses.

The shots

Six photographs

01

Face, front, smiling

Hold the phone at eye level, about one metre away.

02

Face, front, relaxed

Lips relaxed and teeth apart, with no smile.

03

Profile, right side

Head straight, looking ahead.

04

Profile, left side

Head straight, looking ahead.

05

Mouth open, upper teeth

Pull your lips back and show your upper teeth clearly.

06

Mouth open, lower teeth

Pull your lips back and show your lower teeth clearly.

Sending

How to send them

Photographs are medical data too. Do not attach them to the enquiry form. Send them as a password-protected PDF to info@antiparosdental.com, in the same way as your CBCT scan. The password is sent separately.
