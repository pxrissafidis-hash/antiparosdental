Our Story

Two generations, one island, one philosophy.

Antiparos Dental is not just a dental practice. It is the continuation of a family dedication to care — from father to son, with roots on the same island we love.

From a beach on Antiparos

Every story has a beginning. Ours starts on a beach on the island, years ago — a father and a small son who, without knowing it yet, would follow the same path.

Today, the same bond with the island and with dentistry unites two generations under one roof. The father passed on knowledge and experience; the son enriched it with new techniques and international training.

This continuity is the heart of our clinic — and the reason every patient trusts not one, but two doctors who care.
