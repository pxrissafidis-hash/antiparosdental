Our Story

Two generations. One island. One philosophy of care.

It began with the father — for years a scientific associate of the University of Athens. It continues with the son: holder of a master's, doctoral candidate and member of the International Team for Implantology (ITI), with experience in Athens, Paris and Orléans.

The same dedication, from a childhood beach to the clinic of today.
