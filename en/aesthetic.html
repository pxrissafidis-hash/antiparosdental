Cosmetic dentistry

A smile that looks naturally yours.

Aesthetic dentistry is not only about white teeth — it is harmony with your face. Our aim is discreet, natural results that let you smile with confidence.

Design with attention to detail

Every smile is unique. Before any treatment, we study the proportions, the colour and the gum line, so the result suits your features.

We prefer the most conservative solutions that achieve the desired result — preserving as much of the tooth's natural structure as possible.

Natural, not overdone

Our philosophy: a smile should look authentic. We avoid the "artificial" result and aim for harmony that lasts.

Which option fits?

We start from what you want to change

Colour

If you like the shape of your teeth and simply want a brighter smile, whitening is usually the first choice.

Small flaws

For small chips, gaps or asymmetries, composite veneers give a result with minimal intervention.

Shape and colour together

When you want to change the overall look of your smile, porcelain veneers give the most stable result.

We decide on the right option together, after the examination.

Treatments

What we offer

Porcelain veneers

Thin ceramic veneers that correct shape, colour and small gaps. They require removing a thin layer of enamel and are a permanent solution.

Composite veneers

Resin is added directly to the tooth, usually in one visit, with little or no enamel removal. Suitable for small chips, gaps and asymmetries.

Whitening

In the clinic or at home with custom trays. It changes the colour of natural teeth, not of fillings or crowns.

Digital smile design and mock-up

We design your smile digitally and show it to you in your mouth with a mock-up, before anything irreversible is done.

Gum contouring

Reshaping the gum line for a more harmonious, balanced smile.

Aesthetic restorations

Fillings, shape correction and restoration of worn teeth, in a shade that matches your natural teeth.

Our approach

The process

Step by step, with no surprises

01

Examination

Check of teeth and gum health, photographs and a discussion of what you would like to change.

02

Design

Digital smile design and a mock-up in the mouth, so you can see the result before we begin.

03

Preparation

For porcelain veneers: preparation of the teeth, impressions and temporary veneers.

04

Placement

The laboratory needs about 5 to 7 days. Then come try-in, bonding and a check of your bite.

Frequently asked questions

I am coming from abroad. Can porcelain veneers be done in one trip?

Usually, yes. The laboratory needs about 5 to 7 days, so a stay of about ten days is enough in most cases. We set the exact schedule after the examination.

Can I see the result before we start?

Yes. With the mock-up you see the shape and size of your new teeth in your own mouth, before anything irreversible is done.

Is the treatment painful?

Preparation for veneers is done under local anaesthetic where needed. After whitening there may be temporary sensitivity, which passes.

How long do veneers last?

With proper care and regular check-ups, many years. If you clench or grind your teeth, we will suggest a night guard to protect them.

Does whitening also whiten fillings?

No. Whitening only changes the colour of natural teeth. That is why, when fillings are visible, we plan the treatment as a whole.
