Cosmetic dentistry

A smile that looks naturally yours.

Aesthetic dentistry is not only about white teeth — it is harmony with your face. Our aim is discreet, natural results that let you smile with confidence.

[ Photo: clinic / before-after — to be added ]

Design with attention to detail

Every smile is unique. Before any treatment, we study the proportions, the colour and the gum line, so the result suits your features.

We prefer the most conservative solutions that achieve the desired result — preserving as much of the tooth's natural structure as possible.

Natural, not overdone

Our philosophy: a smile should look authentic. We avoid the "artificial" result and aim for harmony that lasts.

Treatments

What we offer

Veneers

Thin ceramic layers that correct shape, colour and small gaps, with a natural result.

Whitening

Safe, controlled whitening for a brighter smile, without harming the enamel.

Aesthetic restorations

Fillings and restorations in a shade that perfectly matches your natural teeth.

Smile design

Complete smile design, taking the face and proportions into account.

Shape correction

Smoothing or reshaping for a more harmonious tooth line.

Wear restoration

Solutions for worn teeth, with an aesthetic and functional result.

Our approach

Health first, then aesthetics

A beautiful smile is built on solid foundations. Before any aesthetic work, we make sure the teeth and gums are healthy — because only then does the result last.

We will explain honestly what is achievable, what your options are and what to expect realistically.
